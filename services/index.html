REMOVALS

Non-licensed asbestos removals across Scotland

Finding asbestos can raise questions about your property, your plans and what to do next. ACE helps homeowners, businesses and property managers understand their options, with clear communication and removal works assessed for the materials and circumstances involved. Based in Glasgow, we support projects throughout Scotland.

Removal is not always the first or only option. Materials in good condition may be better managed in place if they are unlikely to be disturbed. Where removal is appropriate, the starting point is to establish what is present, assess its condition and agree the scope before work begins.

Assessing the right approach

Survey information, sampling results and the proposed work help establish the relevant category of work. Suitable non-licensed tasks can include careful removal of asbestos cement products without substantial breakage. The material alone does not determine the category: its condition, likely fibre release and removal method also matter.

Planning & protection

Non-licensed work still requires a risk assessment, a suitable written plan of work and task-specific training. Appropriate controls must protect workers and others nearby, covering restricted access, dust reduction, protective equipment, decontamination and cleaning as relevant to the job.

Notifiable non-licensed work

Some work falls into the notifiable non-licensed work (NNLW) category. This brings additional duties, including notification to the relevant enforcing authority before starting, designated work areas, medical surveillance and worker health records. The category and requirements must be established before any removal proceeds.

Waste & completion

Asbestos waste needs secure packaging and a controlled disposal route. In Scotland, special waste must be accompanied by the required consignment documentation and taken to a facility authorised to receive it. Discuss the proposed waste arrangements and completion records with ACE as part of agreeing your project.

Clear limits, responsible advice

ACE offers non-licensed removal services and does not hold an HSE asbestos removal licence. Work that requires a licence must be carried out by an HSE-licensed contractor. If the assessment identifies licensable work, ACE will work with you to find the right HSE-licensed removal contractor for your project.

Work involving asbestos in Scotland is governed by the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012. HSE guidance explains the distinction between non-licensed work and notifiable non-licensed work, while SEPA’s special waste guidance explains requirements under the Special Waste Regulations 1996, as amended.

Tell us about your property, any existing survey or sample results and the work you have in mind. We can discuss the next steps and help you understand the scope before you commit to removal.

SURVEYING

Asbestos surveys for properties across Scotland

From day-to-day property management to planned building works, ACE helps you establish where asbestos may be present and what that means for your next step. Based in Glasgow, we provide surveying and sampling support for commercial, industrial and residential properties throughout Scotland.

Management surveys

Identify suspected asbestos-containing materials, their condition and their location to support an asbestos register and the management of a building during normal use.

Refurbishment & demolition surveys

More intrusive surveys focused on the areas affected by proposed works, including materials concealed within the building fabric. We discuss access, the work programme and the survey scope before inspection.

Re-inspections & management updates

Review the condition of previously identified asbestos materials and update the information used to manage them. Changes in condition, occupancy or planned works can inform the next actions.

Sampling & laboratory analysis

Sampling and coordination of laboratory analysis to establish whether suspected materials contain asbestos. Asbestos sample analysis must be carried out by appropriately accredited analysts.

Our focus is on clear findings, an agreed scope and practical recommendations. Survey planning is informed by HSE’s Asbestos: The survey guide (HSG264).

CONSULTANCY

Practical asbestos consultancy, from findings to action

An asbestos report should help you make decisions about your property. ACE supports building owners, property managers, businesses and homeowners across Scotland with advice on survey findings, asbestos management and the options for planned works.

We help clients turn information about asbestos-containing materials into a workable management approach, with support for asbestos management plans, risk assessments and recommendations suited to the property and how it is used.

Management plans & risk priorities

Support with recording asbestos information, identifying priorities, planning condition checks and communicating relevant findings to people who may work on the building.

Ongoing technical support

Advice as circumstances change, including maintenance, refurbishment proposals and re-inspection findings. We help you review existing arrangements and understand when further surveying or specialist input may be needed.

Our advice is informed by the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 and HSE guidance (L143). The duty to manage asbestos covers non-domestic premises and the common parts of multi-occupancy residential buildings. We support dutyholders in understanding the steps relevant to their responsibilities.
